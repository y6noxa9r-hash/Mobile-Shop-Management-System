### 4. Estimation Method
The team uses expert judgment and task decomposition. The project is divided into smaller activities, and each activity receives a duration estimate based on its expected complexity and the work required. Estimates are expressed in working days and assume that assigned team members are available.

### 5. Scheduling Assumptions
- A working day is assumed to contain approximately six focused project hours.
- The schedule excludes official holidays and unexpected absences.
- Design and database activities may proceed in parallel after system design is agreed.
- Implementation estimates depend on the final programming language and database choice.
- Any approved scope change may require the estimates to be reviewed.
