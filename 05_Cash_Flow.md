# Task 3 – Cash Flow
## Mobile Shop Management System

### 1. Purpose
This cash-flow estimate presents the expected initial costs and possible operational savings associated with developing and introducing the Mobile Shop Management System. It is intended for project planning and feasibility discussion.

### 2. Estimation Assumptions
The following figures are **illustrative planning estimates in Egyptian pounds (EGP)**. They are not actual quotations, invoices, confirmed salaries, or financial records from a specific shop.

- The first version is designed for one mobile-phone shop branch.
- Existing computers may be used if they meet the system requirements.
- A barcode scanner and receipt printer are optional.
- The project is estimated to require an initial setup period followed by monthly operation.
- Estimated savings represent time and error reduction; they are not guaranteed cash income.

### 3. Estimated Initial Costs

| Cost item | Estimated cost (EGP) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Development and configuration effort | 12,000 | Estimated project effort |
| Computer or hardware upgrades | 5,000 | Only if existing equipment is insufficient |
| Barcode scanner and receipt printer | 3,500 | Optional devices |
| Initial data entry and staff training | 2,000 | Product and opening-stock setup |
| Testing, backup setup, and contingency | 2,500 | Initial quality and setup allowance |
| **Total initial cost** | **25,000** | Preliminary estimate |

### 4. Estimated Monthly Operating Costs

| Operating item | Monthly estimate (EGP) |
|---|---:|
| Hosting, internet, or related services | 500 |
| Backup and maintenance allowance | 500 |
| **Total monthly operating cost** | **1,000** |

### 5. Estimated Monthly Benefits

| Expected benefit | Estimated monthly value (EGP) | Explanation |
|---|---:|---|
| Time saved in sales and stock tasks | 2,500 | Less manual recording and searching |
| Reduced stock and calculation errors | 1,500 | Better inventory and transaction records |
| Improved purchasing decisions | 1,000 | More accurate stock and sales information |
| **Total estimated monthly benefit** | **5,000** | Indicative operational value |

### 6. Cash-Flow Projection

| Period | Cash inflow / benefit (EGP) | Cash outflow (EGP) | Net cash flow (EGP) | Cumulative cash flow (EGP) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial setup (Month 0) | 0 | 25,000 | -25,000 | -25,000 |
| Month 1 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -21,000 |
| Month 2 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -17,000 |
| Month 3 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -13,000 |
| Month 4 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -9,000 |
| Month 5 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -5,000 |
| Month 6 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | -1,000 |
| Month 7 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | 3,000 |

### 7. Break-Even Estimate
Based on the assumptions above:

- Initial investment = 25,000 EGP.
- Monthly net operating benefit = 5,000 − 1,000 = 4,000 EGP.
- Estimated payback period = 25,000 ÷ 4,000 = **6.25 months**.

Therefore, the initial investment may be recovered during Month 7 if the estimated benefits are achieved consistently.

### 8. Interpretation
The projection indicates that the system may become economically beneficial over time. The largest uncertainty is whether the shop will actually achieve the estimated monthly time savings and reduction in errors. The owner should validate the assumptions using current shop records before making a spending decision.

### 9. Conclusion
The preliminary cash-flow projection is positive in the medium term under the stated assumptions. The figures should be treated as estimates for academic planning and revised after collecting real prices, operating costs, and shop activity data.
