# Current Problem

## Mobile Shop Management System

### 1. Introduction

Our project focuses on the daily management challenges of a mobile phone
shop. For this project scenario, the shop is assumed to rely on manual
records or separate tools to manage products, inventory, sales,
purchases, and supplier information.

### 2. Existing Problems

#### 2.1 Inventory Management

-   It may be difficult to know the exact quantity of each mobile phone
    and accessory.
-   Employees may need extra time to check product availability.
-   Products running low may not be noticed early.

#### 2.2 Sales Management

-   Recording sales manually can take additional time.
-   Manual calculations and data entry may cause mistakes.
-   Finding previous sales transactions may be difficult.

#### 2.3 Product Management

-   Product details may be stored in separate records.
-   Searching for a specific brand, model, or accessory can take time.
-   Updating prices and product information may be inconvenient.

#### 2.4 Supplier and Purchase Management

-   Purchase records may be difficult to organize.
-   Supplier details and received products may not be easy to track in
    one place.

#### 2.5 Reports and Decision-Making

-   Preparing daily or monthly reports manually can take time.
-   The owner may not immediately know which products sell best.
-   Products that need restocking may be difficult to identify.

### 3. Impact

These challenges may lead to wasted time, human errors, poor inventory
visibility, slower customer service, and delayed business decisions.

### 4. Proposed Solution

We propose a **Mobile Shop Management System** that centralizes product,
inventory, sales, purchase, supplier, user, and reporting functions.

### 5. Expected Result

The expected result is to reduce repetitive manual work, improve record
accuracy, save time, and help the shop owner monitor daily operations.

### 6. Project Status

**Current phase:** Problem identification and requirements planning.

The problems above describe the project's working scenario. They should
be checked against the shop's actual workflow before being presented as
verified observations.
