Current Problem
Mobile Shop Management System
1. Introduction
The mobile shop currently faces several daily management challenges. Managing products, inventory, sales, purchases, and suppliers manually can lead to errors, wasted time, and difficulties in organizing business information.
2. Existing Problems
2.1 Inventory Management
Difficulty tracking the exact quantity of available mobile phones and accessories.
Employees may spend extra time checking product availability.
Low-stock products may not be noticed early.
Manual inventory updates can cause mistakes.
2.2 Sales Management
Recording sales manually takes additional time.
Manual calculations may cause errors.
Finding previous sales transactions can be difficult.
Preparing daily sales reports requires extra effort.
2.3 Product Management
Managing product details manually is time-consuming.
Updating prices and product information can be difficult.
Searching for specific products may take extra time.
2.4 Supplier and Purchase Management
Supplier information may be stored in separate records.
Tracking purchase history can be difficult.
Monitoring product purchases manually may lead to missing information.
3. Need for a Management System
The shop needs a centralized management system to organize products, inventory, sales, purchases, suppliers, users, and reports.
The proposed system aims to reduce manual work, improve accuracy, save time, and make daily shop operations easier.
