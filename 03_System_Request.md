# System Request

## Mobile Shop Management System

### 1. Project Sponsor

**Project Sponsor:** Mobile Shop Owner / Store Manager

The shop owner or store manager requests the development of the system
and is responsible for approving the project objectives, providing or
approving resources, setting priorities, reviewing progress, and
confirming whether the proposed system meets the shop's needs.

### 2. Business Need

The mobile phone shop needs a centralized system to organize its daily
operations. Managing products, inventory, sales, purchases, and supplier
information through manual or separate records may cause delays,
duplicate work, and data-entry errors.

The main business needs are to: - Improve inventory management. - Record
sales more accurately. - Reduce repetitive manual work. - Improve
customer service. - Access business information quickly. - Prepare
sales, purchase, and inventory reports.

### 3. Business Requirements

#### Product Management

-   Add, update, remove, and search for products.
-   Store product name, brand, model, specifications, and price.

#### Inventory Management

-   Track available product quantities.
-   Update stock after sales and purchases.
-   Identify low-stock products.

#### Sales Management

-   Record customer transactions.
-   Calculate sale totals.
-   Record payment details.
-   Generate receipts.

#### Supplier and Purchase Management

-   Store supplier information.
-   Record purchases.
-   Update inventory after receiving products.

#### Reports

-   Generate daily and monthly sales reports.
-   Generate inventory reports.
-   Generate purchase reports.

#### User Management

-   Create employee accounts.
-   Assign permissions according to user roles.
-   Limit access to authorized employees.

### 4. Business Value

The system is expected to provide: - Faster checkout and improved
customer service. - More accurate inventory records. - Fewer manual
calculation and recording errors. - Better visibility of product
availability. - Less time spent preparing reports. - Better-informed
business decisions. - More organized daily shop operations.

### 5. Special Issues / Constraints

#### 5.1 Budget

The project must remain within a budget approved by the shop owner.
Possible costs include software development, computers or other
hardware, installation, employee training, and maintenance. The exact
budget has not yet been specified.

#### 5.2 Time

The project should follow an agreed schedule covering requirements
analysis, design, development, testing, data entry, employee training,
and deployment. A confirmed deadline has not yet been provided.

#### 5.3 Legal / Regulatory

Customer and business information must be handled responsibly. Access
should be limited to authorized employees, and applicable privacy and
record-keeping requirements should be reviewed before deployment.

#### 5.4 Integration

The system may need to work with devices or services already used by the
shop, such as computers, barcode scanners, receipt printers, and
supported payment services. Exact integrations must be confirmed.

#### 5.5 Performance

The system should search for products and process sales without
unnecessary delays. It should support the expected number of employees
and daily transactions while keeping inventory records consistent.

### 6. Project Approval

The shop owner or authorized manager should review and approve the
proposed project scope and requirements before the team proceeds to
detailed design and implementation.
